Exp 2 (work in progress): the concept distribution of text Olmo 3 writes unprompted (starting from <|endoftext|>, temperature 1, no top-k or top-p), next to Dolma 3's. In every graph, concepts are ordered by χ in Dolma train (CG paper Eq. 2, all sources), so a concept keeps its place across graphs and frames. Graph 1: Dolma alone (black: train, which sets the order; gray band: held-out V, what noise alone gives). Graph 2: the rollouts alone. Graph 3: both overlaid. Graph 4: graph 3 without the named-concept dots. Bands are the 10th-90th percentile of χ in each bin of 512 ranks, lines the median. Bottom: median log(χ / χ train) in 20 bins; a rollout line further below V at the rare end (right) and above it at the common end (left) means the model's output is more spread than its data. All sets in a frame use the same number of documents, since fewer documents alone steepen the curve.
